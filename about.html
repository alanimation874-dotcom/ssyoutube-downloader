About SSYouTube

SSYouTube was born out of a simple frustration: every existing YouTube downloader was either buried under misleading "Download" buttons, demanded a software install, or throttled speeds unless you paid. We decided to build something genuinely different — a tool that respects your time, your device, and your privacy.

Our platform runs entirely in the cloud. When you paste a YouTube link, our servers handle all the heavy lifting — fetching, decoding, and packaging the video or audio file — and stream the finished result straight to your browser. You never install anything, never grant unusual permissions, and never hand over personal information.

We support every major resolution from 360p up to 4K UHD, and every common audio bitrate from 128 kbps to 320 kbps. Whether you want a lightweight 480p clip for a slow connection or a full 4K download for a large screen, SSYouTube delivers it in one click.

SSYouTube also supports YouTube Shorts — the short-form videos that have become some of the most-watched content on the platform. Save any Shorts clip as MP4 or pull its audio as MP3, just like a regular video.

We are constantly improving. Our engineering team monitors conversion speeds, server uptime, and format compatibility around the clock to ensure SSYouTube remains the fastest and most reliable browser-based downloader available.
